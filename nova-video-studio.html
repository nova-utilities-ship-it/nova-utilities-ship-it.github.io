About Nova Video Studio

Nova Video Studio is an on-device video editing and compression toolkit. Trim clips, resize or change frame rate, convert between formats, extract or remove audio, and compress videos with smart presets — all organized in a Projects workspace that keeps track of your history.

For creators who need to process many clips at once, batch compression and ready-made social media export presets make it easy to prepare a whole folder of videos for upload, plus a one-tap video-to-GIF export for quick sharing.

Features

What it does

Trim & Resize

Cut clips to length and resize video dimensions with ease.

FPS & Format Conversion

Change frame rate or convert between common video formats.

Smart Compression Presets

Shrink file sizes with ready-made presets tuned for quality vs. size.

Batch Processing

Compress or convert a whole folder of videos in one job.

Video-to-GIF Export

Turn any clip into a shareable GIF in one tap.

Audio Extract / Remove

Pull the audio track out of a video, or strip it entirely.

Permissions & Privacy

Nova Video Studio requests storage access to open and export your videos, notifications for background processing updates, and internet access used only for ads.
