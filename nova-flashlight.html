Nova Flashlight

A fast, offline flashlight with steady, strobe and SOS modes, a colorful screen light, and a Morse code transmitter.

About Nova Flashlight

Nova Flashlight turns your camera flash into a reliable torch with steady, strobe and SOS modes, a brightness control on newer devices, and a no-camera-flash fallback that lights up your whole screen instead — handy on devices or in moments where the flash can't be used.

Beyond the torch, it includes a colourful full-screen light with several effects, a Morse code composer that can transmit your message through the torch or screen, a Quick Settings tile, and a home-screen widget — all running without ever requesting camera or photo access.

Features

What it does

Steady, Strobe & SOS

Three torch modes, including an adjustable strobe and SOS signal.

Screen Light Effects

A full-screen colour light with Solid, Rainbow, Police and more effects.

Morse Code Composer

Type a message and transmit it as Morse code through the torch or screen.

Quick Settings Tile & Widget

Turn the torch on from your notification shade or home screen.

Auto-Off Timer

Set the torch to switch off automatically after a chosen time.

No Camera Permission Needed

Controls the flash directly, without ever requesting camera access.

Permissions & Privacy

Nova Flashlight controls your camera's flash directly without ever requesting camera or photo access. It also uses vibration and notification permissions, and internet access solely for ads — it's the only Nova Utilities app with no analytics or crash-reporting SDK at all.
