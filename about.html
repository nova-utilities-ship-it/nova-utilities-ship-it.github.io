About Nova Utilities

Simple tools, built to respect you

A collection of modern offline Android utility applications focused on privacy, performance and simplicity.

Our Mission

Nova Utilities exists to prove that everyday Android tools don't need an internet connection, an account, or your data to be genuinely useful. Every app we build starts from the same question: can this run entirely on-device, fast and privately, without compromising on quality?

That constraint shapes everything — from how we design interfaces to how we choose which apps to build next.

Our Values

What guides the ecosystem

Privacy

No accounts, no unnecessary permissions, and no sending your files anywhere they don't need to go.

Performance

Small, efficient apps that stay fast even on modest hardware and older Android devices.

Simplicity

Every app does one job well, with an interface that gets out of your way.

Offline-First Architecture

Rather than treating offline support as a fallback, we design every app around it from day one. That means core functionality always works, whether you're on a plane, in a basement, or simply prefer not to be connected.

One Consistent Ecosystem

Every Nova Utilities app shares the same design language and interaction patterns, so once you've used one, the rest feel instantly familiar.
