FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Nova Utilities apps work without an internet connection?
Yes. Every app in the Nova Utilities ecosystem is designed to be fully functional offline. Core features never require a network connection.
Do Nova Utilities apps collect my personal data?
No. Files and data you process stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how each app handles permissions and data.
How do I report a bug?
Email contact.novautilities@gmail.com with the app name, your Android version, and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots or screen recordings are extremely helpful.
Can I request a new feature or app?
Absolutely. Send suggestions to contact.novautilities@gmail.com. We track community feedback when planning new releases — see the changelog for what's shipped so far.
When will "Coming Soon" apps be released?
Apps marked "Coming Soon" are actively in development. Follow the Release Notes page for the latest updates on availability.
Are Nova Utilities apps free to use?
Each app's pricing and availability details are listed on its own store page once released. Check the Applications page for current status.
