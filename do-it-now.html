About Do It Now

Do It Now brings together task management, habit tracking, a Pomodoro focus timer, and a calendar-based daily planner in one offline productivity app. Set one-time or recurring tasks with priorities, categories and reminders, and watch your habit streaks build over time.

Progress feels rewarding thanks to XP, levels and achievements, and home-screen widgets keep your tasks and habits visible without opening the app — all backed by a local backup/restore so your data stays yours.

Permissions & Privacy

Do It Now uses notification and exact-alarm permissions only for the reminders and timers you set, plus internet access for ads.
