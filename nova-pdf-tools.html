About Nova PDF Tools

Nova PDF Tools is an all-in-one PDF toolkit for everyday document work — merging and splitting files, compressing bloated PDFs, scanning paper documents straight into a PDF with your camera, and converting between images and PDFs — all processed locally.

Beyond the basics, it includes page-level tools like reordering, deleting and extracting pages, adding page numbers and watermarks, and password-protecting sensitive documents, so you rarely need to leave the app to finish a document task.

Features

What it does

Merge & Split

Combine multiple PDFs into one, or split a large PDF into separate files.

Compress PDFs

Shrink bloated PDF file sizes without noticeable quality loss.

Scan to PDF

Turn a paper document into a PDF with your camera, right from the app.

Image ⇄ PDF Conversion

Convert images to PDF and back again, in either direction.

Page Tools & Watermarks

Reorder, delete and extract pages, and add page numbers or watermarks.

Password Protection

Add or remove a password on sensitive PDFs.

Permissions & Privacy

Nova PDF Tools requests camera access only for the "Scan to PDF" feature, storage access to open and save your PDFs, notifications for background task updates, and internet access used only for ads.
