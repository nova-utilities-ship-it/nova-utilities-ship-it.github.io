About Future Me

Future Me helps you plan for life's big goals — a house, education, retirement, an emergency fund — with SIP and lump-sum investment calculators that show exactly how much you need to save each month to get there.

You can compare different savings scenarios side-by-side, track daily habits alongside your financial goals, and export a PDF report of your plan — all computed and stored entirely on your device.

Features

What it does

Goal Tracking

Track goals like a house, retirement, education or an emergency fund.

SIP & Lump-Sum Calculator

Project investment growth with a year-by-year chart.

Required Monthly Savings

See exactly how much to save each month to hit a target goal.

Scenario Comparison

Compare different savings plans side-by-side with charts.

Daily Habit Tracker

Track habits with streaks alongside your financial goals.

On-Device PDF Reports

Export a PDF report of your plan whenever you want a copy.

Permissions & Privacy

Future Me uses notification permission only for the reminders you set, and internet access for ads. Your goals and financial figures stay on your device unless you choose to export a PDF yourself.

See the full Privacy Policy entry for this app for a complete breakdown of permissions and third-party services.
