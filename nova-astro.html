About Nova Astro

Nova Astro is an astrology and numerology companion. Enter your birth details (place, date and time) to generate a personal birth chart, then explore daily horoscopes, numerology readings, and Panchang (Hindu calendar) data, all calculated locally on your device.

You can save multiple profiles for yourself, family or friends, and check compatibility between any two charts — manual entry is always available if you'd rather not use location services.

Features

What it does

Birth Chart Generation

Build a personal birth chart from your date, time and place of birth.

Daily Horoscope

Get a fresh horoscope reading based on your chart every day.

Numerology Readings

Explore numerology insights calculated from your name and birth date.

Compatibility Matching

Compare two charts to check compatibility between people.

Panchang (Hindu Calendar)

View daily Panchang data alongside your astrological readings.

Multiple Saved Profiles

Save charts for yourself, family or friends and switch between them.

Permissions & Privacy

Location access is optional in Nova Astro, used only if you choose "use current location" when entering your birth place — manual entry always works. The app also uses notifications and internet access for ads.

See the full Privacy Policy entry for this app for a complete breakdown of permissions and third-party services.
