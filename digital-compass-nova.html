About Digital Compass Nova

Digital Compass Nova turns your phone's built-in sensors into a precise compass, spirit level and magnetic field meter. The compass supports both magnetic and true north, with a calibration guide to keep readings accurate.

An optional location screen shows your latitude, longitude and altitude for true-north correction, and the app is fully localized into 8 languages including Hindi and Arabic — all without needing an internet connection for any of its core tools.

Features

What it does

Compass (Magnetic / True North)

Real-time heading from your device's rotation and magnetic sensors.

Spirit Level

Check pitch and roll for level surfaces, with a quick zero reset.

Magnetic Field Meter

Read live magnetic field strength across all three axes.

Optional Location Details

View latitude, longitude and altitude when you choose to share location.

Calibration Guide

A simple guide to keep your compass readings accurate.

8 Languages Supported

Localized into English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi, Arabic and Chinese.

Permissions & Privacy

Location access is optional in Digital Compass Nova, used only for the Location screen and true-north correction. The app also uses vibration and internet access for ads.
