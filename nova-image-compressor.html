About Nova Image Compressor

Nova Image Compressor – Resize & Convert is an on-device photo utility for shrinking file sizes, resizing images, and converting between formats, without ever sending your pictures anywhere. Whether you're freeing up storage, preparing images for the web, or getting a batch of photos ready to share, everything runs locally on your phone.

Alongside compression, it includes a metadata cleaner, rotate/flip/watermark tools, and batch processing for whole folders — so one app covers most of the day-to-day photo cleanup most people reach for several different tools to do.

Features

What it does

Smart Compression

Compress by quality, target file size, or percentage, with a live before/after preview.

Resize & Convert

Resize with aspect-ratio lock and convert between JPG, PNG and WEBP.

Batch Processing

Compress an entire folder of photos in a single pass.

Metadata Cleaner

Strip EXIF and location data from your photos before you share them.

Rotate, Flip & Watermark

Quick edits and a custom watermark, without leaving the app.

Fully Offline

Every compression and conversion happens on your device — nothing is uploaded.

Permissions & Privacy

Nova Image Compressor only requests storage/photo access to open and save the images you choose to process, plus internet access, which is used solely for ads. No account or sign-in is required, and your photos are never uploaded anywhere.
