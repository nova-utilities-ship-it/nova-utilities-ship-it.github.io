Privacy Policy

Straightforward, in plain language — because Nova Utilities apps are built offline-first by design.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Introduction

Nova Utilities ("we", "us", "our") develops offline-first Android utility applications. This Privacy Policy explains what information, if any, our apps and this website interact with, and how that information is handled. This policy applies to the Nova Utilities website and to every application in the Nova Utilities ecosystem, unless a specific app states otherwise in-app.

Our Core Privacy Principle

Nova Utilities apps are designed to work fully offline. Wherever possible, all processing — image compression, file management, document conversion, and similar operations — happens locally on your device. Your files are not uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate servers that receive your app content.

Information We Collect

Through Our Apps

  • Nova Utilities apps do not require account creation or sign-in.
  • Apps do not collect, transmit, or store personal files, photos, or documents you process.
  • Apps may request Android system permissions (such as storage access) strictly to perform the function you request — for example, reading an image you choose to compress.

Through This Website

  • This website is a static site with no backend server, database, or account system.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking scripts to profile visitors.
  • If GitHub Pages hosting infrastructure logs standard technical data (such as IP address for security purposes), that is governed by GitHub's own privacy policy, not ours.

App Permissions

Where an Android permission is required for a feature to work (for example, storage access for a file manager, or media access for an image compressor), the app will request it directly and only use it for the stated purpose. Denying a permission may limit the related feature but will not affect the rest of the app.

Third-Party Services

Nova Utilities apps do not currently integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. If this changes for a specific app in the future, that app's in-app privacy notice and this policy will be updated accordingly before the change takes effect.

Data Security

Because Nova Utilities apps process data locally on your device rather than on remote servers, your files remain under your control at all times. We still recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date to maintain general security.

Children's Privacy

Nova Utilities apps are general-purpose utilities not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This is a living document. As new Nova Utilities applications are released and the ecosystem grows, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated to accurately reflect how each app works. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out at contact.novautilities@gmail.com.
