About Life Inbox

Life Inbox is a one-tap capture app for everything you don't want to lose — text notes, photos, documents, voice memos and links — organized into custom categories and tags so you can find them again later.

Powerful search, reminders with local notifications, and a trash folder that lets you restore deleted items all make it a dependable catch-all, backed by a single-file local backup and restore whenever you want a copy of your own.

Features

What it does

Multi-Format Capture

Capture text notes, photos, documents, voice memos and links.

Custom Categories & Tags

Organize everything with colored categories, tags and favorites.

Powerful Search

Find anything fast, with recent-search history and flexible sorting.

Reminders & Notifications

Set reminders that persist across restarts, with upcoming and overdue views.

Trash with Restore

Deleted items go to Trash first, so nothing is lost by accident.

Local Backup & Restore

Back up your entire inbox to a single file you control.

Permissions & Privacy

Life Inbox requests camera and microphone access only when you choose to capture a photo or voice memo, notification and exact-alarm permissions for reminders, and internet access for ads.

See the full Privacy Policy entry for this app for a complete breakdown of permissions and third-party services.
